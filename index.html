A covenant, not a policy.

Traditional funeral insurance is built around premiums, underwriting, and profit. Our fund works differently: it is owned by its contributors, and every dollar collected for a funeral goes directly to the family in need — contributor or not. There is no markup and no waiting on a claims department — only neighbors covering neighbors, in equal share.

Islam calls for burial without unnecessary delay — ghusl, kafan, and the janazah prayer completed as soon as the family is ready, ideally within a day. That urgency is exactly why this fund is built to move fast: no claims process standing between a death and the funds a family needs, in sha'Allah.

How the fund works

Three steps stand between a grieving family and the support they need.

01

You join the fund

Sign up, add the payment method you'd like charged, and you're a contributor in good standing — no premiums, no medical underwriting.

02

A funeral is needed

Any family in the community facing a funeral — a contributor's or not — can bring the actual cost of the janazah and burial to the fund. The fund divides that cost equally across every active contributor.

03

Everyone contributes their share

Each contributor's stored payment method is charged its equal portion automatically, and the full amount is sent to support the family — usually within a day.

Frequently asked questions

How much will I be charged when someone passes away?

The total verified funeral cost is divided equally among every active contributor. If the funeral costs $10,000 and there are 5,000 active contributors, each contributor is charged approximately $2.

Does the fund only cover contributors' funerals?

No. Any family in the community facing a funeral can request support, whether or not they were a contributor themselves.

Where does the money actually go?

Funds are paid directly to the licensed funeral home or burial-service provider handling the arrangements — never in cash, and never directly to the family.

What happens if my card is declined?

Your share is immediately reassigned across the other active contributors, so the funeral home is always paid in full and on time — the fund never comes up short. You still personally owe your original share, and you'll be reminded by email at 8, 16, 24, and 32 hours to pay it. After 32 hours, UUFFUNDS writes off the unpaid amount rather than pursuing it further. If you do pay after your share was already covered by others, that payment is credited toward your next contribution rather than refunded, since UUFFUNDS doesn't hold a reserve to refund from.

Can I leave the fund at any time?

Yes. You can withdraw at any time. You remain responsible only for shares that were billed before your withdrawal took effect.

What if I think I was charged incorrectly?

You can dispute any charge within 14 days directly from your account's contribution history. Disputing a charge doesn't pause your status — you'll still be charged for other funerals while it's reviewed. An administrator will either uphold the charge or credit the amount toward your next contribution; UUFFUNDS doesn't issue cash refunds since it holds no reserve fund.

Is UUFFUNDS an insurance company?

No. UUFFUNDS is a non-profit mutual-aid arrangement. It does not sell insurance or underwrite risk for profit.

What's the difference between becoming a contributor and making a donation?

Becoming a contributor means joining the equal-share system — you'll be charged your portion whenever a verified funeral cost comes up, same as everyone else. A donation is a one-time gift that goes toward keeping the platform itself running; it's never counted toward any specific funeral and comes with no ongoing obligation.

Can I make a donation without becoming a contributor?

Yes. Donating and becoming a contributor are entirely separate — you can donate on its own, add a donation while signing up as a contributor, or do both. Neither is required to do the other.

How is a death verified before anyone is charged?

A funeral home submits the invoice along with a death certificate and burial permit, linked to the case. An administrator reviews and approves those documents before the case can be charged — no funeral is charged for based on a report alone.

What if someone else already reported the same death?

The system flags likely duplicate reports automatically when the same name comes in twice. An administrator resolves it before either case can be charged, so contributors are never billed twice for the same funeral.

How exactly do donations reduce what I'm charged?

Before a funeral cost is split among contributors, an administrator can apply whatever donations are currently available to reduce the total first. Contributors are only charged their equal share of what's left after that. If enough has been donated, a funeral may be fully covered and no contributor charged at all.

Membership is open to the community. There's no premium to pay today — only your commitment to contribute your equal share when it's needed, for any funeral in the community, not only a fellow contributor's.

Privacy & Security

This is a draft policy for a prototype in development — it is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney familiar with your state's privacy laws (CCPA and similar state statutes) before being published as a live policy.

Information we collect

When you become a contributor, we collect your name, date of birth, phone number, and (optionally) email address, along with a payment method to be charged for your equal share of funeral costs. When someone reports a death, we collect the deceased's name, hospital information, and the reporter's name, phone number, and address. When a funeral home submits an invoice, we collect the funeral home's name and contact information, the invoice amount, and copies of the death certificate and burial permit.

How we use it

We use this information to verify eligibility, calculate and collect equal shares, contact contributors about payment reminders and confirmations, pay funeral homes directly, and keep the records required for accounting and legal purposes. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Who can see it

Contributor and case records are visible only to authorized fund administrators. Funeral home and reporter information tied to a specific case is shared only with the administrators processing that case — not with other contributors or the public.

How long we keep it

Contributor records are retained while you're an active contributor and for a limited period afterward (e.g., 3 years) to satisfy accounting and legal recordkeeping needs, then deleted. Death certificates and burial permits are retained only as long as necessary to verify and process the associated case, then securely deleted. Exact retention periods should be finalized with legal counsel before launch.

Payment information

UUFFUNDS does not store your full card number. Once connected to a real payment processor, card details will be tokenized and handled by a PCI-DSS compliant processor (such as Stripe) — UUFFUNDS' own systems will never see or store raw card numbers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us at UUFFUND@gmail.com.

How this will be kept secure

The production version of this system is designed around these commitments:

  • Encryption in transit: all data submitted over HTTPS/TLS, so it can't be intercepted between your device and our servers.
  • Encryption at rest: personal data stored in an encrypted production database, not in plain text.
  • Individual admin accounts: each administrator will have their own credentials rather than a single shared keycode, with every action logged (who withdrew a contributor, who triggered a charge, and when).
  • Payment tokenization: card data handled entirely by a PCI-compliant processor; never stored directly on UUFFUNDS servers.
  • Data minimization: we only collect what's needed to operate the fund.
  • Access reviews: administrator access is reviewed regularly and revoked promptly when no longer needed.

Prototype status: this demo site does not yet implement server-side storage, encryption, or per-admin authentication. Death reports and invoices submitted here go through your own device's email client rather than a server, and the admin dashboard uses sample data. The items above are the security architecture required before this system handles real contributor data or real payments.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the system develops. Material changes will be communicated to active contributors.
